Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy applies to bookings paid online through PassionRide. Times are counted from the booking's start time (vehicle pickup, stay check-in or tour start), in Indian Standard Time.

1. Vehicle rentals and stays

When you cancelRefund
More than 24 hours before start100% of the booking amount
Within 24 hours of start50% of the booking amount
After the start time, or no-showNo refund

2. Guided tours

When you cancelRefund
More than 72 hours before the tour100% of the booking amount
24 to 72 hours before the tour50% of the booking amount
Within 24 hours, after the start, or no-showNo refund

3. Cancellations by the host or by PassionRide

If a host cancels, the vehicle or stay is not as described, or we cancel for safety or verification reasons, you receive a 100% refund. We will try to offer a comparable alternative.

4. Early returns and shortened stays

Unused days after the start time are not refunded unless the host agrees or the listing was not as described.

5. Security deposits

Where a host requires a deposit, it is returned in full after the vehicle is returned on time and in the same condition, minus any documented damage, fuel shortfall, traffic fines or late fees under the Terms.

6. Pay-at-site bookings

For bookings marked "Pay at Site", no money is collected online. Please cancel in the app if your plans change so the host can re-list.

7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.
