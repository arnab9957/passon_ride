PassionRide User Guide

Your complete guide to booking self-drive vehicles, listing rides for passive income, and hosting guided adventure tours on the PassionRide platform.

1. Welcome & Platform Overview

PassionRide is India's community-driven mobility ecosystem connecting travelers, vehicle owners, and adventure enthusiasts. Whether you want to rent a car or superbike for a weekend getaway, monetize your idle personal vehicle with full escrow protection, or lead an adrenaline-packed group motorcycle convoy, PassionRide delivers a seamless, secure experience.

About Passion Ride

PASS-ON RIDE

Decentralized Peer-to-Peer Mobility & Experiential Tourism Ecosystem

PassionRide was born from a fundamental mobility insight: in India, over 85% of privately-owned vehicles remain parked idle for upwards of 20 hours every day, rapidly losing value through continuous depreciation, insurance dues, and maintenance fees. 2. Vehicle Booking Guide (Renter's Walkthrough)

Renting a vehicle on PassionRide is quick, transparent, and completely digital. From discovery to return, this step-by-step guide explains the entire rental lifecycle for a worry-free self-drive journey.

Live Geolocation Map

Locate available vehicles parked within walking distance of your current location or drop a custom pin anywhere on the live interactive map.

Escrow Protection & Fast Refunds

Rental fares and security deposits are held in institutional escrow. Your security deposit is automatically refunded within hours of trip completion.

Step-by-Step Booking Workflow

1. Discover & Filter Vehicles Nearby

Open the Discovery Map (DiscoveryScreen) or the marketplace feed. Filter listings according to your trip needs:

  • Vehicle Category: Filter across Hatchbacks, Sedans, SUVs, Superbikes, Cruisers, Commuter Motorcycles, Scooters, and Electric Vehicles (EVs).
  • Transmission & Fuel: Select Automatic or Manual transmission; filter by Petrol, Diesel, or Electric range.
  • Instant Booking: Toggle Instant Booking ON to book instantly without waiting for host confirmation.
  • Price Range: Use the interactive slider to align with your daily or hourly rental budget.
2. Review Vehicle Details & Host Trust Score

Tap on any vehicle listing card to inspect its comprehensive profile:

  • High-Resolution Gallery: Examine daylight photos of the front, side, cockpit/dashboard, and boot/helmet storage.
  • Host Reputation: Review the host's Kinetic Trust Score, verified host badge, total trips completed, and reviews from past renters.
  • Included Amenities: Confirm features such as Bluetooth audio, phone mounts, USB chargers, ABS brakes, and complimentary helmets.
3. Configure Trip Dates & Pickup Method

Specify your trip schedule and preferred pickup logistics:

  • Trip Duration: Select pickup date/time and drop-off date/time. Multi-day trips frequently receive automatic discounts of 15% to 25%.
  • Pickup Location: Choose between self-pickup at the host's exact curbside pin or select Doorstep Delivery if offered by the host.
4. Instant KYC & Driving License Verification

Safety is mandatory. First-time renters complete a swift 60-second automated verification:

  • Upload a clear photo of your valid government-issued Driving License (DL).
  • Automated OCR validates license authenticity, expiration date, and vehicle class permissions (LMV for cars, MCWG for geared bikes).
  • Once verified, your profile gains the Verified Driver credential and unlocks instant booking across the app.
5. Transparent Fare Breakdown & Escrow Payment

Review the transparent fare receipt with zero hidden fees before confirming:

  • Base Rental Rate: Transparent hourly or daily rental fee.
  • Trip Insurance: Comprehensive protection covering accidental damages and third-party liabilities.
  • Refundable Security Deposit: Held safely in institutional escrow and returned automatically upon trip completion.
  • Flexible Payment: Pay securely using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, or NetBanking.
6. Pickup & Keyless Smart Lock Unlock

On rental day, use pedestrian map routing in the app to reach the vehicle's exact location:

  • Digital Keyless Entry: If the vehicle features PassionRide IoT, tap "Unlock Vehicle" in your app to unlock doors/ignition keylessly via Bluetooth. For traditional listings, meet the host at the curbside pin for key handover.
  • Mandatory Pre-Trip Photo Inspection: Take 4 clear photos of the vehicle exterior, capture the fuel gauge/battery percentage, and record the initial odometer reading directly inside the app. This protects you against pre-existing scratches!
7. Enjoy Your Journey with 24/7 Roadside Assistance

Hit the open road with complete peace of mind:

  • In-App Host Chat: Message your host for questions about fuel lid levers, Bluetooth syncing, or local road tips.
  • 24/7 Roadside Assistance & SOS: Tap the emergency SOS button on your active trip screen for immediate roadside assistance (flat tires, jumpstarts, or towing).
  • Trip Extension: Need more time? Request a booking extension inside the app before your scheduled drop-off window ends.
8. Vehicle Return & Automated Deposit Release

Returning your rental is quick and effortless:

  • Park at Designated Pin: Return the vehicle to the agreed drop-off pin in a safe, legal parking spot.
  • Fuel Matching: Ensure the fuel or battery level matches the initial pickup reading recorded in the Pre-Trip inspection.
  • Post-Trip Photos: Snap 4 final photos and input the final odometer reading.
  • Lock & End Trip: Tap "End Trip & Lock". Your refundable security deposit is automatically released to your original payment account within 2-4 hours.

Renter Best Practices Checklist

📸 Thorough Pre-Trip Photos

Always take 30 seconds to photograph all four sides and wheels of the vehicle during pickup. The app stores timestamps in the cloud to protect you against unfair damage claims.

⛽ Fair Fuel Matching

Return the vehicle with the same fuel or charge level you received. Refill at local pumps near the drop-off location to avoid host refueling convenience fees.

⏱️ Punctual Returns & Extensions

Late returns delay subsequent renters. If you are running behind schedule due to traffic, request an in-app extension at least 1 hour before scheduled drop-off.

💬 Official In-App Messaging

Always communicate with the host via PassionRide in-app chat. This maintains a verified record of agreements regarding parking pins, keys, or special requests.

3. Vehicle Hosting Guide (Host & Fleet Operator Walkthrough)

This comprehensive guide provides an exhaustive, step-by-step onboarding walkthrough for vehicle owners and fleet operators listing rides on PassionRide. Follow this pipeline to configure vehicle details, verify regulatory documents with automated OCR, pair IoT hardware, and publish to the live peer-to-peer marketplace.

AI-Powered OCR Compliance

Upload Registration Certificates (RC), Insurance policies, and Challan clearances. The integrated OCR engine automatically parses document numbers and expiry dates in seconds.

Keyless IoT & Telematics

Pair OBD-II and GPS telematics to enable digital keyless entry, tire pressure monitoring (TPMS), live battery/fuel state, and automated odometer logging.

Video Walkthrough: Host Listing Wizard Open on YouTube

Step-by-Step Hosting Workflow for New Users

1. Pre-Listing: Account Onboarding & Kinetic Trust KYC

Before listing your first vehicle, you must establish an authenticated host profile with verified trust credentials:

  • Sign In / Authenticate: Authenticate via native email or Google OAuth (using PassionRide's hybrid popup flow).
  • Profile Setup: Ensure your legal full name, phone number, and avatar photo are configured in the ProfileScreen.
  • Kinetic Trust Verification: Navigate to DocumentsComplianceScreen to upload a valid government ID or Driving License. Passing KYC unlocks your verified host badge and enables vehicle publishing.
Trust Score Impact: Hosts with complete KYC and Kinetic Trust scores > 90% receive up to 3x higher booking visibility on the discovery map.
2. Navigate to the Host Listing Wizard

Access the vehicle registration gateway directly from the application interface:

  • Open the bottom navigation bar or side drawer and select Host Fleet Dashboard (ProviderDashboardScreen, Navigation Index 8).
  • Tap the primary action button "Register New Vehicle" or the floating action button "+".
  • The app routes seamlessly into the Host Listing Wizard (RegisterVehicleScreen, Navigation Index 10).
3. Fill Basic Vehicle Information & Geolocation

Define the identity, category, and exact pickup pin for your rental listing:

  • Make & Model Title: Enter a recognizable name (e.g., "Bajaj Pulsar N250", "Royal Enfield Hunter 350", or "Hyundai Creta SX").
  • Vehicle Category: Select from Motorcycle, Scooter, Sedan / Car, SUV, or Electric.
  • VIN / Registration Number: Provide the official registration mark (e.g., WB11442A).
  • Vehicle Description: Detail maintenance condition, accessories provided (dual-channel ABS, helmet, phone mount), and handover instructions.
  • Pickup Geolocation:
    • Option A: Live GPS Tap "Use Live GPS" to automatically lock the exact coordinates of your current host location.
    • Option B: Map Pin Picker Tap "Pick Pin on Map" to open the interactive Google Map dialog (InteractiveMapPinPicker) and drop a pin at the precise garage or curbside pickup spot.
4. Curate Vehicle Photo Gallery & Cloud CDN Sync

Showcase high-resolution imagery to maximize rental conversions:

  • Multi-Photo Selection: Tap "Add Multiple Photos" to pick multiple shots from your device gallery simultaneously.
  • Live Camera Capture: Tap "Take Camera Photo" to capture real-time exterior and interior condition shots.
  • Web Image URLs: Enter verified image URLs or tap preset photography cards for instant demonstration media.
  • Set Cover Photo: Tap any photo in your uploaded gallery to promote it as the primary cover image (first index displayed to renters).
  • ImageKit Cloud CDN Upload: Photos are asynchronously compressed and transferred to ImageKit CDN (/vehicles directory) for instantaneous low-latency global delivery.
5. Configure Specifications, Fuel & Seating

Specify the mechanical parameters so renters find matches tailored to their driving license and trip requirements:

  • Fuel Type: Select from Petrol, Diesel, Electric (EV), or Hybrid. (Selecting Electric automatically enables EV battery SoC indicators).
  • Transmission: Select Manual or Automatic.
  • Seating Capacity: Set the certified passenger count (e.g., 2 seats for motorbikes, 5–7 seats for cars).
6. Set Rental Rates & Instant Booking Policy

Configure your revenue model and reservation workflow:

  • Daily Rental Price (₹ / $): Enter the competitive 24-hour rate (e.g., 500.00 per day). Dynamic hourly pricing is computed automatically.
  • Enable Instant Booking:
    • ON (Recommended): Pre-verified renters with passing Kinetic Trust scores can book and unlock immediately without waiting for host confirmation.
    • OFF: Each booking request sends a push alert requiring manual host review and approval before confirmation.
7. Pair IoT Telematics & Keyless Digital Smart Lock

Integrate vehicle telemetry for digital keyless handover and live diagnostics:

  • Hardware Pairing: Connect your vehicle's OBD-II port adapter or onboard GPS tracker to the PassionRide IoT gateway.
  • Telemetry Node Initialization:

  "locked": true,
  "engineOn": false,

  • Hardware Ping Test: Tap the "Test Lock / Unlock" button directly inside the wizard to confirm two-way communication between the client and vehicle actuator.
8. Upload & Verify Mandatory Regulatory Documents (AI/OCR)

PassionRide enforces complete legal compliance to protect hosts and renters under applicable motor vehicle acts. Upload scans in PDF or JPG/PNG format:

Document Type Required Fields Automated OCR Verification Mandatory?
Vehicle Registration (RC) RC Number, Registration Expiry Date, Document Scan Scans for standard RTO registration formats and expiry threshold (> current date). Yes (Required)
Challan Clearance Challan Reference Number, Clearance Date, Document Scan Verifies clearance date within 30-day validity window with zero pending impounds. Optional / Recommended
Insurance Policy Policy Number, Provider Name (e.g. HDFC ERGO), Policy Expiry Date, Document Scan Validates active third-party or comprehensive policy coverage dates. Yes (Required)

When you select a document, DocumentOcrService.processDocument() analyzes the file in real-time, extracts text strings, auto-populates input fields, and tags the document with a verification confidence score.

9. Publish Listing & Instant Marketplace Activation

Tap "Publish Vehicle Listing". The platform orchestrates the following backend operations:

  • Uploads document scans to ImageKit CDN under /compliance_documents.
  • Inserts records into Supabase compliance_documents table with initial status Verified or Action Required.
  • Persists the listing to the Supabase vehicles table with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) linking the vehicle to your hostId.
  • Your vehicle immediately appears on the live Discovery Map, category filters, and search queries for verified renters worldwide.
10. Post-Listing: Fleet Management & Earnings Payouts

Once active, manage every aspect of your vehicle's lifecycle from the host portals:

  • Provider Dashboard (ProviderDashboardScreen): Toggle vehicle availability across Available, In Service, Rented, or Maintenance states with a single tap.
  • Keyless Booking Verification: During vehicle pickup, renters scan a dynamic QR code or submit a 6-digit OTP PIN via BookingVerificationScreen to unlock doors/ignition digitally.
  • Telematics Hub (TelematicsHubScreen): Live-track vehicle GPS coordinates, check OBD-II diagnostic fault codes, monitor speeding alerts, and inspect battery/fuel levels while on trip.
  • Earnings & Payouts (EarningsScreen): Track gross rental fees, escrow deposits, platform commissions, and initiate bank payouts via UPI or direct bank transfer.

Host Best Practices & Operational Checklist

📸 Photography Standards

Upload at least 4 clear photos: 3/4 front exterior, side profile, cockpit/dashboard with odometer, and clean interior or helmet storage. Bright daylight photos achieve 40% more booking conversions.

📍 Accurate Geofencing

Always verify that your pickup pin matches the exact street or garage location. Renters use in-app pedestrian routing to find the vehicle for keyless pickup.

🛡️ Document Validity Thresholds

Renew vehicle insurance and fitness certificates at least 15 days before expiration. The platform automatically pauses listings if insurance documents expire.

⚡ Response Time & In-App Chat

Respond to renter inquiries within 15 minutes through the built-in real-time chat (ChatScreen). Quick responses increase your Kinetic Trust rating and repeat rental bookings.

4. Guided Tour Hosting Guide (Tour Organizer Walkthrough)

Turn your passion for motorcycling, road trips, and route exploration into an organized travel business. PassionRide empowers verified tour leaders, riding clubs, and local guides to publish curated group adventures, manage rider rosters, and collect secure bookings with escrow protection.

Convoy Coordination & Live Roster

Publish multi-stop itineraries with waypoint checkpoints, mandatory safety gear requirements, and live participant rosters with integrated rider group chat.

Guaranteed Escrow Payouts

Participant fees are secured in platform escrow when riders book. Payouts are transferred automatically to your registered bank account upon tour completion.

Step-by-Step Tour Creation Workflow

1. Access the Tour Registration Wizard

Launch the guided tour publishing tool inside PassionRide:

  • Open the bottom navigation or side menu and select Register Guided Tour (RegisterTourScreen, Navigation Index 11).
  • You can also tap "Host Tour" from the Provider Dashboard or use the AI Tour Generator (AiTourGeneratorScreen) to instantly brainstorm routes and descriptions.
2. Define Tour Title, Theme & Category

Craft a captivating headline and select the appropriate adventure classification:

  • Tour Category: Choose from Mountain Passes & Ghats, Coastal Cruises, Off-Road Dirt Trails, Heritage & Cultural Rides, Weekend Breakfast Getaways, or Track Days.
  • Tour Headline: Give your ride an evocative name (e.g., "Western Ghats Monsoon Odyssey: 3-Day Coastal & Cloud Ride").
  • Cover Image: Upload a striking, landscape-oriented photograph showcasing the scenic destination or convoy riding formation.
3. Map Out Itinerary, Waypoints & Checkpoints

Structure the journey schedule so participants understand the route pacing:

  • Start Location & Assembly Point: Define the exact GPS departure spot (e.g. iconic landmark with ample parking and fuel access).
  • Waypoints & Halts: Add scheduled rest stops, scenic photography viewpoints, breakfast/lunch dhaba stops, and fuel refill stations.
  • Destination & Route Distance: Provide total kilometers and estimated daily riding hours to set realistic endurance expectations.
4. Set Convoy Capacity, Dates & Terrain Difficulty

Establish group safety parameters and rider skill expectations:

  • Rider Group Size: Specify minimum required participants (to confirm departure) and maximum limit (e.g., 8–15 riders to keep convoys manageable).
  • Departure Date & Duration: Choose single-day morning ride or multi-day expedition dates.
  • Riding Difficulty: Rate terrain difficulty: Beginner-Friendly (smooth highways, gentle pace), Intermediate (twisty hill roads, moderate gravel), or Advanced / Pro (high altitudes, steep passes, water crossings).
5. Specify Vehicle Eligibility & Mandatory Safety Gear

Clear rules ensure convoy harmony and rider safety:

  • Vehicle Type: State whether riders Bring Their Own Vehicle (BYOV), or if rental vehicle packages are available through PassionRide partner hosts.
  • Minimum Engine Capacity: Specify displacement thresholds if highway cruising speeds require 250cc+ or 350cc+.
  • Mandatory Protective Gear: Clearly stipulate ISI/DOT full-face helmets, armored riding jackets, knee guards, riding boots, and gloves.
6. Transparent Pricing & Package Inclusions

Configure pricing and clearly delineate what participants receive:

  • Per-Person Ticket Price: Set registration fee in INR (₹) or USD ($).
  • Included Perks: Checkmark package inclusions: Safety Marshal Escort, Support Sweep Vehicle, Luggage Transfer, Breakfast/Refreshments, Puncture Repair Kit & Mechanic Backup, First-Aid Kit, Forest/Permit Entry Fees, and Action GoPro Photos.
  • Exclusions: Clearly communicate what riders must pay out of pocket (personal fuel, alcohol, hotel room upgrades).
7. Convoy Safety Protocols & In-Trip Communication

Tour safety is the hallmark of a top-rated host:

  • Lead & Sweep System: Assign a Lead Rider (sets steady pace, navigates turns) and a Sweep Rider (remains behind the last participant to assist stragglers).
  • Tour Group Chat: When riders register, they are automatically added to the private in-app Tour Group Chat for coordination, packing checklists, and live updates.
  • Emergency Contingency Plan: Outline nearest medical trauma centers and roadside towing contacts along the journey path.
8. Publish Tour & Manage Confirmed Riders

Launch your tour to PassionRide's eager travel community:

  • Tap "Publish Guided Tour". Your tour instantly appears on the Discover Tours feed and AI itinerary recommendations.
  • Track registrations, review participant profiles and trust scores, and answer questions through group chat.
  • On tour completion, mark the trip finished. Tour earnings are deposited directly into your linked bank account via UPI or NEFT.

Tour Host Best Practices & Convoy Safety

🧭 Pre-Ride Route Reconnaissance

Always pre-scout your itinerary within 7 days of departure to check for unexpected road closures, monsoon landslides, bridge repairs, or detour conditions.

📢 Mandatory Pre-Departure Briefing

Dedicate 10 minutes at the assembly point to review stagger riding formation, braking distances, hand signals (hazard left/right, slowdown, single file), and overtaking etiquette.

🧰 Sweep Vehicle & Tool Support

For tours exceeding 100km, arrange a sweep vehicle carrying tubeless puncture kits, portable 12V inflators, jumpstart cables, engine coolant, and spare fuel canisters.

⭐ Build Rider Loyalty & Badges

Take high-quality group photos at scenic viewpoints and share them in the tour chat. Great experiences turn one-time participants into loyal repeat riders who boost your Kinetic Trust rating.

5. Safety, Trust & Support Ecosystem

At PassionRide, community safety is our paramount priority. Every booking, host vehicle listing, and guided tour is shielded by automated verification algorithms, bank-grade escrow, and dedicated 24/7 emergency response infrastructure.

Kinetic Trust™ Scoring

A multi-factor reputation algorithm that computes user trust scores from verified government KYC, trip completion history, prompt communication, and genuine peer reviews. High trust unlocks instant bookings and lower deposit thresholds.

Automated Escrow Protection

All financial transactions—including rental fees, security deposits, and tour bookings—are secured in institutional escrow. Hosts receive payouts seamlessly after trip conclusion, while renter security deposits are refunded automatically.

24/7 Emergency SOS & Roadside Help

Encountered a flat tire, dead battery, or unexpected mechanical breakdown? Tap the emergency SOS button inside your active trip screen for immediate dispatch of on-site recovery mechanics, jumpstarts, or flatbed towing.

IRSARGO AI Assistant Support

Have questions while navigating the app? Tap the floating IRSARGO AI assistant in the bottom corner of any screen to get real-time context-aware answers regarding vehicle specs, booking policies, or local driving laws.
